03 / METHOD

From pixels to observed cycles.

Offline evaluation uses Python MediaPipe Solutions pose landmarks and camera-plane knee angles. M45 adds an interpretable temporal motion rule. The web demo uses separate MediaPipe Tasks Vision and has not been established equivalent on publisher video.

  1. 01Video frames

    Authorized Squat TRAIN MP4 clips at controlled nominal observation cadence.

  2. 02Pose landmarks

    Offline MediaPipe Solutions supplies hip / knee / ankle tracking.

  3. 03Joint geometry

    Estimate a 2D knee angle; handle insufficient visibility and tracking-side changes.

  4. 04Relative excursion

    Require a high-angle anchor, enough lowering, then sufficient recovery.

  5. 05Event matching

    Frozen one-to-one matching of predicted events against annotated GT ends.

  6. 06Paired evidence

    Compare original M39 vs M45 and resample 20 whole TRAIN video pairs.

05 / LIMITATIONS & OPEN QUESTIONS

What we cannot claim.

01 · NO OFFICIAL TEST SCORE

Publisher TEST remains untouched. These TRAIN F1 scores are not an independent generalization estimate.

02 · IDENTITY UNVERIFIED

Filenames are disjoint, but subject, session, collection site and camera independence are unknown.

03 · NO ZERO-REP CONTROLS

Publisher Squat TRAIN lacks count-zero clips. False alarms during non-repetition motion remain unmeasured.

04 · BROWSER PARITY OPEN

Offline Python MediaPipe Solutions outcomes cannot be assigned to the browser Tasks Vision demo.

05 · ORIGIN & LICENSE OPEN

Matching video bytes to a local TAR does not authenticate its source or confirm redistribution permissions.

06 · LABEL SEMANTICS OPEN

Valid bounds and video clocks do not prove human GT cycle ends are aligned with observed knee-angle phases.

06 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Read the sources. Challenge the claims.

W5 contains static, aggregate frozen TRAIN evidence. Owner-local JSON reports, archive, private video, per-video predictions and landmarks are not packaged into this public frontend. M53 normalized MAE, OBO and official TEST results are not claimed or invented.
